# M0.1G — Animation and audio report
Date: 2026-11-20. Branch: `feat/m0-1g-animation-audio`.

## Baseline
- Spec taken from `origin/main` commit `7f7ee83` (`tasks/M0.1G-ANIMATION-AUDIO.md`).

## Delivered
### G1 presentation FX (`src/fx.c`)
- Lightweight motion list: deal cascade, flip squash, settle bounce, slide trails.
- Held-card motion breadcrumbs.
- Warm-gold selection glow (legality still hue-tinted).
- Soft menu fade wash on screen changes.
- Restrained victory foundation shimmer/cascade (fireworks remain as fallback when FX disabled).
- `--no-anim` disables FX entirely; input/rules never block on animations.

### G3 SFX (`src/audio.c`, `assets/runtime/sfx/`)
- SDL2 software mixer (no SDL_mixer dependency).
- Original deterministic WAVs generated in-repo (no third-party samples):
  - `shuffle_riffle.wav` — multi-burst riffle ending with soft deck-squaring taps
  - `deck_tap.wav`, `place.wav`, `draw.wav`, `invalid.wav`, `win_chime.wav`, `menu.wav`
- Opening/new-game plays shuffle + deck tap before/with deal intro FX.
- `--no-audio` disables playback; missing devices fail soft.

### G2 music
- Not committed. Spec requires user-approved licensed shortlist before shipping music assets.
- Audio subsystem is ready for future music once approved.
