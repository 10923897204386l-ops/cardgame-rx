## Deliverables in reviewable stages
### F1 — Art source and comparison audit
- Capture actual 640×480 current game screenshot (controls bar shown/hidden, tall fan, selected card) and compare with the visual direction. Identify precise discrepancies before editing.
- Audit current Kennard SVG/PNG assets and existing rasterization pipeline, licences and manifest. Reuse original CC0 Kennard source where possible; never hand-copy ranks, suits or pip positions from generated mockup. Preserve accurate 52-card identities and court card art.
- If new decorative art is needed, use explicitly licensed assets or create original art with provenance and keep editable source and raster versions. Do not overwrite locked title image.

### F2 — Cards
- Preserve current 72×100 logical card size, tableau overlap geometry and card index/suit readability unless an objective layout defect requires a documented adjustment.
- Faces: subtle warm ivory paper tone, crisp high-contrast black/red indices and pips, classic Kennard court illustrations, restrained black/charcoal keyline, rounded corners. Ensure card rank/suit remain legible in exposed narrow strips and that no aliasing obscures pips.
- Backs: rich royal/deep blue with gold tropical floral ornamentation, high contrast and symmetric repeatable design; keep navy/wine alternate back options working if existing settings depend on them. A new back can be default only after native-resolution review.
- Add subtle offset/contact shadows to visible card stacks, including held stack, without hiding focus, legality outline or invalid-drop red flash. Avoid per-frame costly blurs; prerender or use simple deterministic SDL compositing.

### F3 — Felt and decoration
- Brighten toward richer emerald-green felt with fine-scale woven/grain texture, subtle gradient/vignette and gentle lighting. Avoid harsh noise, moire or distracting high-frequency patterns on RG35XXSP.
- Peripheral hibiscus/plumeria and a *few* separate fallen pink petals with subtle contact shadows; decorative clusters remain in corners and outside main card and foundation regions. No flower directly under rank/suit strips, selected outlines or controls labels. Avoid a busy floral wallpaper effect.
- Prefer a 640×480 native background with source artwork kept separately; ensure graceful crop/scale and no UI text baked into background.
- Match existing warm cream HUD and Permanent Marker lettering, but do not change them unless required for contrast.

### F4 — Validation and handoff
- Validate with native 640×480 real application captures: default new deal, tall tableau, held cards, invalid move, controls shown/hidden, alternate card back. Show side-by-side before/after screenshots in PR or report.
- Run full desktop and SDL-free CTest regressions, asset manifest/hash/license checks, clean build, image loading error path, and memory/performance smoke tests. Do not claim hardware validation.
- Update README, manifest, PROJECT_STATE.md, ROADMAP.md, and tasks/M0.1F-REPORT.md. Commit changes in small logical steps: asset sources, raster generation, rendering, tests/docs.
- Open squash-merge PR for review; **do not merge automatically**.
