# M0.1G — Card animation, transitions, MIDI soundtrack and sound effects

Status: proposed implementation handoff, 2026-11-20. Begin only after confirming M0.1F art PR is merged; do not disrupt current artwork. User requests polished physical card motion, UI transitions, multi-instrument open-source Hawaiian/surf MIDI music, card swoosh/flip/error sounds and win applause.

## G1 — animation architecture and movement
- Preserve deterministic Klondike rules and existing atomic transactions. Animation is a presentation queue driven by committed game-state transitions, not a second game state. Undo reverses visual motion without altering rule semantics. No source ghosts or double moves.
- Introduce animation event types: initial deal, lift, held stack movement, legal drop, turn-over, invalid move, undo, victory cascade, menu fade/slide. Source/destination card coordinates and affected card IDs captured at transaction boundaries; bounded queue and cancellation/skip support.
- Proposed timings at 60fps target: pick-up lift 90–130ms, move/drop 160–240ms, turnover 180–240ms, invalid nudge 110–160ms, staggered initial deal 35–65ms between cards, menu transitions 120–200ms; tune to native 640×480 performance and avoid excessive bounce. Ease-out movement and subtle drop settle; flipping cards scales width around center with a face swap at midpoint (avoid incorrect faces). Whole carried stacks remain aligned. Shadows follow cards.
- Input responsiveness: gameplay cannot apply a second move to an unsettled transaction; permit buffered directional input where safe, cancellation/fast-forward for deal and win, and no key-repeat-induced duplicate commits. Expose animation speed (Normal/Fast/Off) if minimal to implement. Ensure reduced-motion mode fully bypasses transitions.
- Test navigation, undo during animation, rapid pick/drop, invalid drops, stock draw/recycle, pause/resume, restart, resize/quit, tall fans and win.

## G2 — MIDI soundtrack selection and audio backend
- **Only multi-instrument arrangements**. Seek 3–5 varied tracks in Hawaiian lounge (steel guitar/ukulele/bass/percussion) and 1960s surf instrumental (clean electric guitar/bass/organ/drums). Avoid solo piano/ukulele, vocals, jarring or aggressively distorted tracks.
- Research upstream MIDI composition licences **per file**, not merely the hosting site. Verify explicit CC0, CC-BY, MIT or similarly redistributable rights, composition author, original MIDI arranger, attribution requirements and SHA256. Do not assume a public-domain song makes a third-party MIDI arrangement freely redistributable. If no qualifying suitable Hawaiian/surf multi-track MIDIs are found, **stop and present candidates/gap**, do not quietly substitute unrelated tracks or bundle copyrighted material.
- Candidate research source: Keynata Commons https://carf-coder.github.io/keynata-commons/ offers CC0 MIDI and rock categories, but no specific track has yet been auditioned or approved. No track is preapproved.
- MIDI playback can be evaluated with FluidSynth (LGPL-2.1) and a legally redistributable SoundFont. For RG35XXSP prioritize **offline rendering approved MIDI to compact OGG or equivalent** and SDL2 audio playback to avoid shipping a large soundfont or depending on hardware MIDI synthesis. Preserve original .mid sources and track-specific attribution in assets/music/source, generated compressed audio in assets/music/rendered, and reproducible render commands/checksums. If the device's SDL2 build lacks OGG support, choose and document a tested low-footprint format. Never depend on desktop system SoundFont being available on handheld.
- Music loops gracefully with no audible clicks, offers shuffle/track selection if easy, pauses/ducks appropriately for menu/win, and has music volume and mute. Test silent fallback when files are missing.
- Licensing and performance evidence required before including music in distribution.

## G3 — sound effects
- Soft airy swoosh for moving cards, crisp paper flip, muted drop/stack tap, quiet unobtrusive invalid-move tick, menu select/cancel, short applause on win. Distinct but cohesive. Avoid excessively frequent sounds on D-pad navigation.
- Source/generate original SFX or obtain clearly licensed CC0/CC-BY assets; record provenance, credits and hashes. Normalize levels; avoid clipping when multiple events overlap. Provide SFX volume and mute independent of music.
- Trigger sound only on committed or explicitly invalid UI action, not duplicate per animation frame. Win applause once per completed game, including after undo/re-win only per explicit policy.
- Test silent/no-audio-device fallback and mute state.
